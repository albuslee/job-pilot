The Role 🥳
We are seeking Senior Engineers to join our AI team and build the next generation of AI-powered products.
This is a
high-impact applied AI role
for engineers who want their work to matter. You’ll build and ship real AI features that solve customer problems across multiple industries - turning cutting-edge agentic AI into systems customers rely on in production.
This is not a ML or MLOps role. You’ll work across the full software stack to design, build, and ship AI-powered workflows, tools, and experiences. Your work will span prompt and context engineering, agent orchestration, backend services, APIs, infrastructure, and user-facing interfaces - often within the same project.
You’ll be part of a small high-performing team of AI specialists reporting directly to our co-founder and be deeply embedded across our various Product & Engineering teams. If you’re excited by LLMs, multi-agent systems, and solving hard, real-world AI problems, this role is built for you.
Your impact💥
Develop Agents and Multi-agent systems: Design and implement AI agent and extend existing agents with new capabilities. This includes managing the agent’s context (using techniques like sub-agents, retrieval based context management, sliding context windows, etc.).
Develop Tools for agents: Develop complex tools for agents like computer use, browser use, etc.
Multi-agent orchestration and tool calling: Research and develop systems on how agents should collaborate, and how agents should call tools.
Fullstack development: Build and maintain production-grade APIs and AI-powered features, from backend services to user-facing experiences.
Evaluate and improve: Evaluate AI’s performance using evaluations and tests to then iterate on prompts, agent tools and agents orchestration to improve output quality and reliability.
